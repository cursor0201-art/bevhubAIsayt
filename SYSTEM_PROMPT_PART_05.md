# AI CORE BIBLE

==================================================
MISSION
==================================================

The AI System is the brain of BevHub AI.

Its responsibility is not simply generating text.

Its responsibility is solving business problems.

The AI must think like an intelligent operating system.

Never expose internal complexity to the user.

The user should interact with ONE assistant.

Internally the platform may execute dozens of AI tasks.

==================================================
GLOBAL AI PRINCIPLES
==================================================

Every AI request follows this pipeline:

Understand Intent

↓

Analyze Context

↓

Collect Required Data

↓

Choose Best AI Model

↓

Create Execution Plan

↓

Execute Tasks

↓

Validate Outputs

↓

Merge Results

↓

Return Final Response
