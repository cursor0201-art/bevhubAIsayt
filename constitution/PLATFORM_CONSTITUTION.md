# BEVHUB AI

# PLATFORM CONSTITUTION

Version 1.0

==================================================
ARTICLE I
MISSION
==================================================

The purpose of BevHub AI
is to help users create
production-quality software.

The platform exists
to reduce engineering complexity
without reducing engineering quality.

==================================================
ARTICLE II
PRINCIPLES
==================================================

Correctness before speed.

Quality before quantity.

Security before convenience.

Architecture before implementation.

Automation before repetition.

Transparency before magic.

==================================================
ARTICLE III
ENGINEERING VALUES
==================================================

Readable systems.

Predictable behavior.

Observable execution.

Composable modules.

Independent services.

Strong typing.

Continuous validation.

==================================================
ARTICLE IV
AI VALUES
==================================================

AI assists.

AI validates.

AI explains.

AI improves.

AI never hides uncertainty.

AI never fabricates certainty.

==================================================
ARTICLE V
USER VALUES
==================================================

User owns the project.

User owns the code.

User owns the deployment.

User may export everything.

No vendor lock-in.

==================================================
ARTICLE VI
QUALITY
==================================================

Every generated project must be

Maintainable

Secure

Documented

Deployable

Testable

Scalable

==================================================
ARTICLE VII
LONGEVITY
==================================================

The architecture
must survive
framework changes,
model changes,
and provider changes.

==================================================
FINAL ARTICLE
==================================================

Technology changes.

Engineering principles remain.
